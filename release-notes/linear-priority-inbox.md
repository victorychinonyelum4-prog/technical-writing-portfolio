# Priority Inbox

Not every notification needs your attention right away. Priority Inbox surfaces the most important notifications while keeping less urgent ones out of the way.

Linear uses personalised settings and predictable logic to determine which notifications are important to you. Users can customise their notifications using the filter feature or by choosing the notification source in settings.

## Inbox view

The new priority inbox section lets users customise their notification display to their preferences.

To access the priority inbox, click on the “inbox” on the left sidebar, or you can simply press `G` then `I` to go to the inbox from any page that you’re on.

Scroll through the inbox using `J` / `K` or `↑` / `↓` while you're in the inbox or a single notification.

## Inbox actions

From the notification settings, you can manage various kinds of actions such as deleting, unsubscribing, or even editing the notification (issue) properties. Manage some actions with keyboard shortcuts or through the use of settings.

Snooze a notification by selecting (right-clicking) the notification, then click `H`. To delete a message — select the notification you want to delete — then click on `Backspace` to remove the notification from your inbox. Delete all read notifications by simply clicking Shift + Backspace.

You can automatically read or unread selected messages by simply using the `U` button or `Alt/Option` + `U` to mark all notifications as read.

If you want to unsubscribe from a notification source, Press Shift + S, or use the Unsubscribe option inside the notification. Existing notifications from that source will remain in your inbox; unsubscribing only stops new ones.

>Note: actions apply only to the notification(s) you selected.

## Customising your view

Before customizing your Priority Inbox, make sure you're inside the inbox. Open Display options  (found in the top-right corner of your inbox) to update the properties of your notifications. You can toggle **Show snoozed** or **Show unread first.**

Because reminders remain visible until you act on them, there's no toggle to hide reminders in Display options.

Choose the notifications, badge count, and grouping options you want to include in your priority inbox.
